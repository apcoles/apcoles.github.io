01 — Overview

Project Overview

Context

Our mobile banking app was experiencing high churn rates and low engagement. User research revealed that customers found the app confusing and struggled to complete basic tasks like transferring money or paying bills.

Goal

Redesign the mobile banking experience to improve usability, increase user engagement, and reduce customer support inquiries. Our target was to achieve a NPS score of 50+ and reduce task completion time by 30%.

02 — Problem

The Challenge

Through user interviews and analytics, we identified several critical pain points:

Complex Navigation

Users needed 5+ taps to complete basic tasks. Navigation structure was unclear and inconsistent.

Low Discoverability

Key features were hidden. 70% of users were unaware of available features.

Poor Feedback

Lack of clear status indicators and error messages caused confusion and repeated support tickets.

03 — Research

Understanding Our Users

Research Methods

Qualitative Research

  • • 15 user interviews
  • • 8 usability testing sessions
  • • Customer support ticket analysis

Quantitative Research

  • • Analytics review (50k+ users)
  • • A/B testing on key flows
  • • Survey responses (n=500)
04 — Solution

Design Solutions

Based on our research insights, we implemented a comprehensive redesign focused on simplicity, clarity, and user empowerment.

Simplified Information Architecture

Restructured navigation to reduce the number of taps needed for common tasks. Implemented a bottom navigation bar with clear labels and icons.

Quick Actions Dashboard

Created a customizable home screen with one-tap access to frequently used features like transfers, bill pay, and account details.

Improved Feedback System

Added clear success confirmations, progress indicators, and helpful error messages to guide users through each action.

Enhanced Visual Hierarchy

Redesigned UI with consistent typography, spacing, and color usage to improve scannability and reduce cognitive load.

06 — Learnings

Key Takeaways

Start with User Research

The most impactful design decisions came directly from user interviews and usability testing. Investing time upfront in research saved weeks of development time.

Iterate Based on Data

Continuous A/B testing and analytics monitoring allowed us to validate assumptions and make data-driven refinements throughout the project.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Close collaboration between design, engineering, and customer support teams was crucial for identifying problems and implementing effective solutions.
